Federation guide & tour

This is a guide and a tour, not a live AI chat. It walks you through the desktop in order. Nothing here answers back.

1. The four doors. Tools, Guides, Maps, Systems — the opening video at the top of the Federation shows the map. Tools is live now, at next-xus.net. Guides and Maps are not built yet.
2. The desktop. The nebula is the background, always behind everything. Tap any tile and a small mini browser pops up with it inside. A second small pop-up next to it shows where to get it or buy it. Move windows by the gold title bar, shrink them with –, make them big with ▢, close them with ×.
3. The informational line. The row of cards above the tiles is a summary of every category. Tap a card and it takes you to that row — no mini browser pops up.
4. The meeting room. ChatGPT and Grok open in mini browsers, side by side. The video call with Roger is Roger's link to add.
5. The throne. The operational throne and the Senate. Roger Sim Legacy sits on the throne; Aria sits there as default; Pontus is regent, always available.
6. The business. The YAML database is the brain. The Real Agency Kit is free. Offspring Seeds are $49 for a Ring of 6 and $69 for their own Ring of 12.
